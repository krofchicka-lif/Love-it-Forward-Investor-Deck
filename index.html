Appendix E · Compliance and legal

LIF is a software layer. Not a financial institution.

LIF never touches money or card data. All stored value, payments, and settlement remain with platform partners. As a result, LIF does not require stored-value licensing, money transmitter licenses, PCI compliance, KYC or AML programs, or escheatment and breakage liability.
Platform partners manage
Funds · compliance · PCI · chargebacks · fraud detection
LIF manages
User experience · forwarding logic · chain tracking · impact analytics
We are the emotional commerce layer, not the payment processor.

Appendix J · Why platforms won't build this

Platforms move money.
Love it Forward moves people.

Why platforms won't build it
Behavioural virality is cross-brand, not platform-specific. It requires neutrality platforms can't offer. It introduces emotional and social dynamics outside core commerce KPIs. This is not a feature. It's a new behavioural layer.
Why platforms want LIF
Merchants acquire customers without ads. Platforms see higher GMV, retention, and merchant satisfaction. No cannibalization of payments, fees, or data ownership. When merchants win, platforms win.
We are complementary infrastructure, not competition. We don't optimise for one ecosystem.
